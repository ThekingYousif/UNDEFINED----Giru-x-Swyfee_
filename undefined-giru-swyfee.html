Chapter One

The Final Round

The neon lights of the arena flickered overhead as the announcer's voice echoed over the loudspeakers: "Final round. Elimination match."

In the center of the stadium stood the two players everyone talked about—the deadliest duo in the competitive scene. Giru, the hyper-calculating strategist, and Swyfee_, the lightning-fast, high-octane mechanical prodigy.

Together, they were an unstoppable force. They dominated every gamemode, outsmarted top-tier pros, and flexed multiple talents that left their opponents completely helpless. It didn't matter if it was a high-stakes survival mode or a brutal PvP showdown—if Giru and Swyfee_ marked you as a target, your match was already over.

"What's the play, Giru?" Swyfee_ asked, adjusting his headset with a cocky grin.

Giru didn't break focus, his eyes tracking the enemy team's movement on the radar. "They're trying to pinch us from the flank. Standard bait-and-switch. You take the high ground, draw their fire, and I'll execute the trap from the shadows."

"Say no more," Swyfee_ smirked.

In a flash, Swyfee_ lunged into the open, moving with terrifying speed. The enemy squad took the bait, unleashing a hail of fire. But Swyfee_'s movement was poetry in motion—dodging, weaving, and parrying every shot with micro-second precision. Just as the pros closed in for what they thought was an easy kill, Giru stepped out from the flank.

With a single, flawlessly timed command, Giru locked down their escape routes. The trap snapped shut. Swyfee_ flipped back into position, lining up the final shots side-by-side with Giru.

Eliminated.

The crowd went wild, but the duo barely blinked. They shared a unwritten rule that went deeper than code or tactics: if one falls, the other can't survive. They fought as a single mind, two halves of a perfect weapon.

Giru cracked his knuckles and looked over at Swyfee_. "Ready for the next lobby?"

"They aren't ready for us," Swyfee_ replied. Whatever it takes, the deadly duo remains undefeated.
